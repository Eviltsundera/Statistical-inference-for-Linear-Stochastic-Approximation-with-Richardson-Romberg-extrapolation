## Short recommendation

The best near-term archival target remains ACC 2027, with manuscripts due 2026-09-11 (joint ACC + L-CSS option) or 2026-09-25 (regular ACC). The only CORE A* option with a concrete near-term deadline is now ICLR 2027 (abstract 2026-09-11, paper 2026-09-16); like the former AAAI plan, it requires framing the result as theory for stochastic approximation in ML/RL/optimization and is too soon if proof gaps remain. FSML 2026 stays attractive as a non-archival submission and now has an extra month (deadline 2026-09-30).

For a more natural archival ML/statistics paper, the realistic cycle is still AISTATS 2027 / ALT 2027 / COLT 2027 / UAI 2027 / ICML 2027. AISTATS 2027 (Paris, 2027-04-26 to 2027-04-28) should open its call with a deadline around early October 2026; ALT 2027 is expected in the same window; COLT 2027 (Tokyo, 2027-06-28 to 2027-07-02) should have a deadline around February 2027; ICML 2027 deadlines are expected in late January 2027 (aggregators list 2027-01-16 abstract / 2027-01-22 paper, unofficial).

## Reputation ranking

Among the listed venues, the most prestigious broad ML/theory targets are:

1. COLT / ICML / ICLR / AISTATS. COLT is the most theory-selective; ICML and ICLR are the most visible broad ML venues (both CORE A*), with ICLR's reviewer pool somewhat less theory-focused than COLT's; AISTATS is probably the best statistical-ML match for an inference theorem.
2. UAI / ALT / AAAI. UAI is strong for uncertainty and probabilistic/statistical ML; ALT is specialized and respected in learning theory; AAAI is broad, visible, and selective, but its 2027 window has passed — it is relevant again only for AAAI-28 (deadlines expected around summer 2027).
3. ACC. Very respectable in control and systems, and probably the best reputable near-term fit for stochastic approximation / Markovian linear recursions. It is not as prestigious as COLT/ICML/AISTATS from an ML-theory perspective, but it is a serious archival venue.
4. L4DC. Young but increasingly respected single-track venue at the ML/dynamics/control interface with PMLR proceedings; very strong topical fit for stochastic approximation with Markovian data, but less career-legible than the A*/A venues above.
5. FSML workshop. Scientifically relevant and useful for feedback, but non-archival and less career-legible than a main conference paper.
6. ICSMD / ICMSA / ECSO-CMS. Potentially fine for presentation or backup, but much less prestigious for this thesis topic than the venues above.
7. AIMLSystems. Respectable as a systems-oriented venue, but the fit is weak for a mostly mathematical thesis; its 2026 deadline has passed anyway.

Practical interpretation: if the goal is maximum prestige, aim for AISTATS/ALT/COLT/UAI/ICML after polishing, or ICLR 2027 if a strong ML-facing version can be ready by mid-September. If the goal is a reputable and realistic first archival submission on the current timeline, ACC 2027 is the best target. If the goal is early feedback without consuming the archival result, FSML 2026 is useful.
