
\section{A Depth-One RR Misadjustment Bound and Its Limitation}

This subsection records why the depth-one route is insufficient. The final
Berry--Esseen assembly uses the depth-two Levin transfer.

A depth-one route would control
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
D_1^{\text{mis, RR}}
  = \frac{\sqrt{n}}{n - n_0} \sum_{k=n_0}^{n-1}
    (2 J_k^{(1, \alpha)} - J_k^{(1, 2 \alpha)}),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with centered part
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
D_{1, \text{c}}^{\text{mis, RR}}
  \coloneqq D_1^{\text{mis, RR}} - \mathbb{E} D_1^{\text{mis, RR}}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Assume \(2 \alpha \le \alpha_\infty\) and \(2 \alpha \le \alpha_{\text{inv}}\).

By Lemma~\ref{lem:levin-prop-2},
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_\pi \left[J_\infty^{(1, \alpha)}\right] = \alpha \Delta + O(\alpha^2),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
and the linear term cancels in the RR combination. Hence
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\lVert \mathbb{E} D_1^{\text{mis, RR}} \rVert \le C \sqrt{n} \, \alpha^2.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(p, \alpha) \coloneqq p^{3 / 2} \, t_{\text{mix}}^{1 / 2} / a
                + p^{1 / 2} \, t_{\text{mix}}^{3 / 2} \sqrt{\alpha / a}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
For \(w \in \left\{\alpha, 2 \alpha\right\}\), Lemma~\ref{lem:last-shifted-first-order} gives
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\lVert u^\top (T_n^{(1, w)} - \mathbb{E} T_n^{(1, w)}) \rVert_{L_p}
  \le C \lVert u \rVert \, \alpha \, \Phi(p, \alpha),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
after increasing \(C\). Since
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
u^\top J_k^{(1, w)}
  = ((I - w \overline{A})^{-\top} u)^\top T_k^{(1, w)}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
the inverse bound yields, uniformly in \(k\),
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\lVert u^\top \left(2 J_k^{(1, \alpha)} - J_k^{(1, 2 \alpha)})
     - \mathbb{E} (2 J_k^{(1, \alpha)} - J_k^{(1, 2 \alpha)}\right) \rVert_{L_p}
  \le C \lVert u \rVert \, \alpha \, \Phi(p, \alpha),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
and PR averaging gives
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\lVert u^\top D_{1, \text{c}}^{\text{mis, RR}} \rVert_{L_p}
  \le C \lVert u \rVert \, \sqrt{n} \, \alpha \, \Phi(p, \alpha)
  = O(\sqrt{n} \, \alpha).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Together with the bias estimate,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\lVert u^\top D_1^{\text{mis, RR}} \rVert_{L_p}
  \le C \lVert u \rVert \, \sqrt{n} \, \alpha \, \Phi(p, \alpha)
    + C \lVert u \rVert \, \sqrt{n} \, \alpha^2.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
At \(\alpha \asymp n^{-1 / 2}\) the centered term is \(O(1)\), so this route does
not yield the target \(n^{-1 / 4}\) Berry--Esseen remainder.
