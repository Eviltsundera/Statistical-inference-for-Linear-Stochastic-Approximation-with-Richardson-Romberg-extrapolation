\section{\texorpdfstring{Norm Estimate for $H_j^{(n)}$}{Norm Estimate for H}}

To bound $\lVert H_j^{(n)}\rVert$ we use the following standard result.

\begin{lemma}[Lyapunov contraction]
  \label{lem:lyapunov-contraction-local}
  Let $-\overline{A}$ be a Hurwitz matrix. Then for any
  $P=P^\top\succ0$ there exists a unique $Q=Q^\top\succ0$ satisfying
  \[
    \overline{A}^\top Q+Q\overline{A}=P.
  \]
  Let
  \[
    a:=\frac{\lambda_{\min}(P)}{2\lVert Q\rVert},
    \qquad
    \kappa_Q:=\frac{\lambda_{\max}(Q)}{\lambda_{\min}(Q)},
  \]
  and
  \[
    \alpha_\infty
      :=\min\left\{
        \frac{\lambda_{\min}(P)}
             {2\kappa_Q\lVert\overline{A}\rVert_Q^2},
        \frac{\lVert Q\rVert}{\lambda_{\min}(P)}
      \right\}.
  \]
  Then for all $\alpha\in[0,\alpha_\infty]$,
  \[
    \alpha a\leq\frac12,
    \qquad
    \lVert I-\alpha\overline{A}\rVert_Q^2\leq1-\alpha a.
  \]
\end{lemma}

In the RR estimates below we assume explicitly that $0<\alpha$ and
$2\alpha\leq\alpha_\infty$. Hence the Lyapunov contraction applies at both
step sizes $\alpha$ and $2\alpha$.

For $1\leq j\leq n-1$, combine the triangle inequality,
submultiplicativity, the norm equivalence, and the Lyapunov contraction at step
sizes $\alpha$ and $2\alpha$:
\[
  \begin{aligned}
    \lVert H_j^{(n)}\rVert
      &\leq\kappa_Q\sum_{i=1}^{n-j}
        (1-\alpha a)^{(i-1)/2}
        (1-2\alpha a)^{(n-j-i)/2} \\
%     &= \kappa_Q(1-\alpha a)^{(n-j-1)/2}
%        \sum_{k=0}^{n-j-1}
%        \left(\frac{1-2\alpha a}{1-\alpha a}\right)^{k/2} \\
      &\leq\kappa_Q(1-\alpha a)^{(n-j-1)/2}
        \frac{1}
        {1-\sqrt{(1-2\alpha a)/(1-\alpha a)}}.
  \end{aligned}
\]
For $\alpha a\leq1/2$ the geometric rate is bounded below by an
\emph{elementary} inequality: combining
\[
  \sqrt{\frac{1-2\alpha a}{1-\alpha a}}
    \leq\sqrt{1-\alpha a}
\]
(since $1-2\alpha a\leq(1-\alpha a)^2$) with
$1-\sqrt{1-x}\geq x/2$ on $[0,1]$ gives
\[
  1-\sqrt{\frac{1-2\alpha a}{1-\alpha a}}
    \geq\frac{\alpha a}{2},
  \qquad
  \frac{1}{1-\sqrt{(1-2\alpha a)/(1-\alpha a)}}
    \leq\frac{2}{\alpha a}.
\]
Defining the chapter-local norm-equivalence constant $K_Q:=\kappa_Q$
(distinct from the Assumption~2 sup-norm constant $C_A$, which enters
separately via $\lVert\overline{A}\rVert$ in the next subsection), we arrive
at the final estimate
\[
  \lVert H_j^{(n)}\rVert
    \leq K_Q(1-\alpha a)^{(n-j-1)/2}\frac{2}{\alpha a}.
\]
The kernel decays geometrically in $n-j$ at rate $\sqrt{1-\alpha a}$ but its
summed weight is of order $1/(\alpha a)$. The next subsection keeps the
remaining powers of $a$ explicit when this kernel is multiplied by the
prefactor $\alpha^2$ in $\Delta J_n^{(0,\alpha)}$.
