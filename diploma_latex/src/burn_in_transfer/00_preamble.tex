

This chapter transfers the stationary $n_{0} = 0$ Berry--Esseen bound to the
deterministic-start Richardson--Romberg average after burn-in.

For deterministic starts we strengthen the stationary condition by adding the
startup/product-stability restrictions:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{\mathrm{st}}(p)
  := \min (
    \alpha_{\mathrm{prod}}(p),
    \alpha_{\mathrm{prod}}(2p),
    \frac{1}{2 a}
  ),
\quad
\alpha_{\mathrm{burn}}(p,q)
  := \min (
    \alpha_{\mathrm{stat}}(q),
    \alpha_{\mathrm{st}}(p)
  ).
\label{eq:alpha-admissibility-thresholds}
\end{equation}
Throughout this chapter, the burn-in small-step condition is
$2 \alpha \le \alpha_{\mathrm{burn}}(p,q)$.
