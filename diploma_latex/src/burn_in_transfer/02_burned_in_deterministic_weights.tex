
\section{Burned-in Deterministic Weights}

With the $\sqrt{m}$ normalization, the depth-zero linearized term has the
weights
\begin{equation}
Q_{l; n_{0}, n}^{(\alpha)}
  := \alpha \sum_{k = \max\left\{n_{0}, l\right\}}^{n - 1} B_{\alpha}^{k - l},
\quad
1 \le l \le n - 1,
\label{eq:burn-Q-alpha}
\end{equation}
and $Q_{l; n_{0}, n}^{(\alpha)} = 0$ for $l \ge n$. The RR weight is
\begin{equation}
Q_{l; n_{0}, n}^{\mathrm{RR}}
  := 2 Q_{l; n_{0}, n}^{(\alpha)}
     - Q_{l; n_{0}, n}^{(2 \alpha)}.
\label{eq:burn-Q-RR}
\end{equation}
The leading burned-in sum is
\begin{equation}
W_{n,n_{0}}^{\mathrm{RR}}
  := -\frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} \sum_{l = 1}^{n - 1}
      Q_{l; n_{0}, n}^{\mathrm{RR}} \varepsilon(Z_{l}).
\label{eq:burn-W-RR}
\end{equation}
